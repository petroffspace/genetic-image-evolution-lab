# De-Noising & Anti-Grain Fix Plan for `genetic-image-evolution-lab`

## Overview & Problem Statement

Some images and animations generated by `lab` (`main.go`) exhibit **noisy / grainy / sandpaper / fibrous regions** or **high-frequency temporal boiling**. While `renderVersion = 4` introduced canvas supersampling (`supersampleFactor`, 2×–4×) to reduce sub-pixel palette aliasing on small previews, **six distinct mechanisms in the synthesis, texturing, shading, and quantization pipeline still inject high-frequency spatial grain or temporal shimmer** into the 1024×1024 synthesis tile (`fieldTile`) and final RGBA output.

In empirical measurements on a 9-cell grid rendered at `320×180`, noisy cells showed a 2nd-derivative Laplacian-to-gradient ratio (`lap / grad`) up to **`1.52`** and mean pixel-to-pixel gradients up to **`34.98`** (compared to `lap / grad <= 1.13` and `grad ~ 5–12` on clean, smooth cells).

---

## Root Causes in `main.go`

### 1. Shallow Spectral Exponents (`Exponent`, `ExponentHi`) & Wide `BandLimit` Roll-Off
- **Where:**
  - `randomGenome` (`main.go:1554-1568`): initializes `Exponent` in `[1.5, 3.5]`, `ExponentHi` in `[1.5, 3.5]`, `BreakFreq` in `[0.15, 0.65]`, and `BandLimit` in `[0.3, 0.9]`.
  - `breedGenome` (`main.go:1742-1748`) & `Layer` mutation (`main.go:1839-1842`): allows `Exponent` and `ExponentHi` to mutate all the way down to **`0.5`**, and `BandLimit` up to **`1.0`**.
  - `synthChannel` / `synthPair` (`main.go:2361-2382`, `2490-2495`, `2560-2565`): computes amplitude `amp = 1.0 / math.Pow(f, e/2.0)` with Gaussian roll-off `math.Exp(-((f - cutoff)^2) / (2 * cutoff^2))`.
- **Why it causes grain:**
  - On a 2D `1024×1024` FFT tile (`maxFreq = 512`), a 2D amplitude spectrum $f^{-e/2}$ has radial power $2\pi f \cdot f^{-e} = 2\pi f^{1-e}$. Whenever $e \le 2.0$ (and especially $e \in [0.5, 1.8]$), high frequencies carry **equal or greater variance per octave** than low frequencies—producing pink/blue 2D sand noise.
  - Furthermore, the Gaussian `BandLimit` falloff has $\sigma = \text{cutoff}$. When `BandLimit >= 0.5` ($\text{cutoff} \ge 256$), frequencies right up to Nyquist ($f = 512$) retain $e^{-0.5} \approx 61\%$ of their amplitude.

### 2. Line Integral Convolution (`Lic`) Integrating Raw White Noise at Fine Widths
- **Where:**
  - `randomLic` (`main.go:637-656`), `licTile` (`main.go:660-753`), `breedGenome` (`main.go:1801-1819`).
- **Why it causes grain:**
  - `licTile` generates a grid of raw `cellHash` white noise of size `nN = 512 >> Width` (`main.go:671`):
    - `Width = 0` (30% of random LICs): `512×512` white noise grid (1-pixel hair).
    - `Width = 1` (35% of random LICs): `256×256` white noise grid (2-pixel hair).
  - After streamline integration (which can be as short as `Length = 8` in `randomLic` or `Length = 2` in `breedGenome`), `licTile` calls `standardize(out)` (`main.go:751`), forcing the high-frequency streak texture to **unit standard deviation (`1.0`)** before mixing up to `Mix = 0.60` (or `1.0` after mutation) into the base structure.
  - Across streamline perpendiculars, adjacent streamlines have uncorrelated white-noise seeds, creating harsh pixel-to-pixel high-frequency fibrous grain (`grad = 34.98, lap = 52.92` in cell 4).

### 3. Relief Shading (`ReliefStrength`) Differentiating Post-Transform Creases & Noise
- **Where:**
  - `randomGenome` (`main.go:1563`): `ReliefStrength` up to `1.2`.
  - `breedGenome` (`main.go:1751`): `ReliefStrength` mutates up to **`2.0`**.
  - `colorizeFloat` (`main.go:3128-3143`) and legacy `colorizeField` (`main.go:2965-2975`).
- **Why it causes grain:**
  - In `colorizeFloat` (`main.go:3138-3143`), `lum01 := applyTransform(float64(lum[idx]), cfg)` is computed first, and then `dxl, dyl` are finite-differenced from raw `lum[idx ± reliefSpan]` and added as `cfg.ReliefStrength * reliefGain * (dxl*lx + dyl*ly)`.
  - Spatial differentiation multiplies every spatial frequency component $f$ by $2\pi f$. Any high-frequency energy in `lum` (from low `Exponent`, `Lic`, `RD`, or `Worley` cracks) is amplified linearly with frequency into gritty sandpaper embossing.

### 4. Unfiltered High-Frequency Chroma Modulation (`chromaCfg`)
- **Where:**
  - `spectralField` -> `chromaCfg` (`main.go:2731-2746`).
- **Why it causes grain:**
  - `chromaCfg()` copies `cfg` and sets `chCfg.BreakFreq = 0` (`main.go:2734`).
  - Because `BreakFreq` is zeroed, the chroma field (`Seed + 9091`) **ignores `ExponentHi`** and uses `cfg.Exponent` all the way to Nyquist ($f = 512$). If a genome has a smooth high-frequency breakpoint (`Exponent = 1.6, ExponentHi = 3.4, BreakFreq = 0.2`), its luminance is smooth at high frequencies, but its **chroma field remains rough ($e = 1.6$) at high frequencies**, injecting fine color grain into `t` (`t += cfg.ChromaStrength * f.chroma[idx]`, `main.go:3146`) right before the palette lookup!

### 5. Steep Terrace Steps (`Transform == 3`) + Multi-Cycle Palettes (`PalC`) + Ordered Dither (`bayer8`)
- **Where:**
  - `applyTransform` (`main.go:2228-2239`): `TerraceLevels` up to `15` (random) or `24` (bred), with steep step transition `smoothstep01((frac - 0.5) / 0.2)` (slope $5\times$ per terrace level, or up to $120\times$ total gain!).
  - `quantizeDither` (`main.go:3092-3108`, `3153-3155`): adds `bayer8[y&7][x&7]` 8×8 periodic dither to every pixel.
- **Why it causes grain:**
  - When `TerraceLevels` is high (`> 10`), small high-frequency ripples in `t` cross terrace boundaries repeatedly, creating jagged, noisy contour rings.
  - When downstream filters or encoders amplify local contrast, `bayer8`’s periodic 8×8 pattern becomes visible texture in flat/smooth gradients.

### 6. High-Frequency Temporal Boiling in `flowTurns` & `morphGenes` (Animations)
- **Where:**
  - `flowTurns` (`main.go:1447-1453`): `k := math.Round(strength * math.Sqrt(f/4))` with random sign `cellHash(1, x, y, 9) < 0.5`.
  - `morphGenes` (`main.go:1375-1418`): swings `Exponent` and `ExponentHi` down to `0.5` during live `Morph` animation.
- **Why it causes temporal grain/boiling:**
  - High-frequency bins ($f \sim 256$) spin $8\times$ faster in random opposing directions than low-frequency bins ($f \sim 4$), making fine surface detail "boil" or sizzle rapidly from frame to frame.
  - In `morphGenes` (`main.go:1377-1380`), `g.Exponent` and `g.ExponentHi` can dip by `-0.25 * s` down to `0.5` mid-loop, temporarily making the animation grainy halfway through a cycle.

## Concrete Fixes to Implement in `main.go`

### Fix 1: Enforce Smooth High-Frequency Spectral Decay (`synthChannel`, `synthPair`, `randomGenome`, `breedGenome`, `morphGenes`)
1. **Raise Exponent Floors:**
   - In `randomGenome` (`main.go:1555, 1564`):
     - Change `Exponent` range from `1.5 + rng.Float64()*2.0` to `1.8 + rng.Float64()*1.8` (`[1.8, 3.6]`).
     - Change `ExponentHi` range from `1.5 + rng.Float64()*2.0` to `2.4 + rng.Float64()*2.0` (`[2.4, 4.4]`) so high frequencies always roll off steeply.
   - In `breedGenome` (`main.go:1742, 1747, 1839, 1840`, and reset at `1949`):
     - Raise `Exponent` clamp floor from `0.5` to `1.8` (`clampF(..., 1.8, 4.5)`).
     - Raise `ExponentHi` clamp floor from `0.5` to `2.2` (`clampF(..., 2.2, 10.0)`).
   - In `morphGenes` (`main.go:1377, 1379, 1404`):
     - Raise `Exponent` clamp floor from `0.5` to `1.8` and `ExponentHi` floor from `0.5` to `2.2`.
2. **Tighten `BandLimit` Roll-Off in `synthChannel` & `synthPair` (`main.go:2375-2382`, `2560-2565`):**
   - Cap `BandLimit` in `randomGenome` / `breedGenome` to `[0.08, 0.65]` (instead of `1.0`).
   - Steepen the Gaussian roll-off above `cutoff` from `2 * cutoff * cutoff` to `0.5 * cutoff * cutoff` (i.e. $\sigma = 0.5 \cdot \text{cutoff}$), or apply a hard cosine taper before Nyquist (`f > 0.75 * maxFreq`), so Nyquist-scale pixel sand is always suppressed.

### Fix 2: Smooth the Chroma Modulation Field (`spectralField` -> `chromaCfg`, `main.go:2731-2746`)
- In `chromaCfg()` (`main.go:2731-2746`), instead of leaving `chCfg.Exponent` raw when `chCfg.BreakFreq = 0`:
  ```go
  chCfg.BreakFreq = 0
  if chCfg.Exponent < 2.6 {
      chCfg.Exponent = 2.6
  }
  if chCfg.BandLimit <= 0 || chCfg.BandLimit > 0.35 {
      chCfg.BandLimit = 0.35
  }
  ```
- Note: Because `synthPair` (`main.go:2424-2435`) shares `BandLimit` between `cfgA` and `cfgB`, either keep `chCfg.BandLimit = cfg.BandLimit` and set `chCfg.Exponent = math.Max(cfg.Exponent, 2.8)` + `chCfg.BreakFreq = cfg.BreakFreq` + `chCfg.ExponentHi = math.Max(cfg.ExponentHi, 3.2)` (which `synthPair` already supports per-channel via `exponentOf(ch.cfg, f)` at `main.go:2490`!), so `synthPair`’s single-FFT optimization stays 100% intact while making chroma silky smooth!

### Fix 3: Tame `Lic` (Line Integral Convolution) White-Noise Grain (`main.go:637-753`, `1801-1812`)
1. **Eliminate 1-pixel (`Width = 0`) raw white-noise grids:**
   - In `randomLic` (`main.go:645-654`) and `breedGenome` (`main.go:1810`) and `licTile` (`main.go:671`), clamp `Width` to `[1, 3]` (or `[2, 3]`) so noise cells are at least `2–4` tile units wide instead of single-pixel white noise.
2. **Minimum streamline integration length & cross-streamline smoothing:**
   - Raise minimum `Lic.Length` in `randomLic` (`main.go:640`) to `14 + rng.Float64()*26` and in `breedGenome` (`main.go:1802`) from `2` to `12`.
   - Cap `Lic.Mix` in `randomLic` (`main.go:641`) to `0.15 + rng.Float64()*0.25` and in `breedGenome` (`main.go:1803`) to `[0, 0.45]`.
   - In `licTile` (`main.go:740`), apply a 1-pixel periodic box blur (`boxBlurPeriodic(lic, m, 1)`) before upsampling and calling `standardize(out)` so adjacent streamlines blend smoothly instead of forming 1-pixel salt-and-pepper ridges.

### Fix 4: Smooth Relief Gradient Sampling & Cap `ReliefStrength` (`main.go:1563, 1751, 3128-3143`)
1. **Cap `ReliefStrength`:**
   - In `randomGenome` (`main.go:1563`): `rng.Float64() * rng.Float64() * 0.5` (was `1.2`).
   - In `breedGenome` (`main.go:1751`): clamp `ReliefStrength` to `[0.0, 0.6]` (was `2.0`).
2. **Increase `reliefSpan` minimum footprint in `colorizeFloat` (`main.go:3128-3143`):**
   - Currently `reliefSpan := maxInt(int(reliefGain+0.5), 1)`. Using `maxInt(int(reliefGain*1.5+0.5), 2)` averages the central difference over a wider baseline (`2*reliefSpan >= 4` pixels), acting as a natural low-pass derivative filter that shades macro-contours without embossing pixel-level grain.

### Fix 5: Soften Terrace Transitions (`applyTransform`, `main.go:2228-2239`)
- In `randomGenome` (`main.go:1561`) and `breedGenome` (`main.go:1749`), cap `TerraceLevels` to `[2.0, 12.0]` (was `24.0`).
- In `applyTransform` (`main.go:2237`), widen the smoothstep transition band from `0.2` to `0.35`:
  ```go
  edge := smoothstep01((frac - 0.5) / 0.35 + 0.5)
  ```
  (Note: also notice in `main.go:2237` that `smoothstep01((frac - 0.5) / 0.2)` maps `frac = 0.5` to `0` and `frac = 0.7` to `1`, asymmetric around `0.5`; centering it as `(frac - 0.325) / 0.35` or `0.5 + (frac - 0.5)/0.35` makes terrace bands symmetric and anti-aliased.)

### Fix 6: Tame High-Frequency Temporal Boiling in `flowTurns` (`main.go:1447-1453`)
- In `flowTurns` (`main.go:1447-1453`), cap the frequency multiplier so high-frequency bins do not spin wildly faster than mid-frequency bins:
  ```go
  func flowTurns(x, y int, f, strength float64) float64 {
      effF := math.Min(f, 36.0) // cap spin rate above 36 cycles/tile
      k := math.Round(strength * math.Sqrt(effF/4))
      if cellHash(1, x, y, 9) < 0.5 {
          k = -k
      }
      return k
  }
  ```

### Fix 7: Penalize High-Frequency Grain in `measureImage` & `imageQuality` (`main.go:7586-7669`)
- Currently `imageQuality(st imageStats)` (`main.go:7651-7658`) rewards `st.detail` (`q *= clampF(st.detail/0.03, 0.5, 1)`) with **no upper penalty** when `st.detail` is excessive (noisy/grainy).
- Add a high-frequency grain penalty in `imageQuality`:
  ```go
  if st.detail > 0.08 {
      q *= clampF(1.0 - (st.detail-0.08)/0.12, 0.2, 1.0)
  }
  ```
  This causes `seedPopulation` ("Generate All") and verification retries to automatically reject overly grainy/noisy candidates during scouting.
